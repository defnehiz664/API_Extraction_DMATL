2. von mises total strain calculation is wrong.
    the total reduction cannot be obtained by adding the percent thickness reduction from different processes,
    but the rule should be instead:
        1/ln(1-((1-r1)*(1-r2)*(1-r3)))
    the total percent reduction should be instead:
        1-((1-r1)*(1-r2)*(1-r3))
